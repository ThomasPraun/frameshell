# Cloud transcription: an optional paid provider plugin behind a per-run consent gate

#382 asks for transcription through a cloud service next to whisper.cpp, optional and simple for the person and the agent. The owner chose the first service and the consent rules on 2026-11-18 (epic #451, wave 24, question Q2). This ADR adds a decision; it does not change SPEC decision 10 (transcription is first-class, whisper.cpp is the default provider) or decision 39 (voiceover).

## Context (verified)

- `TranscriptionProvider` (`packages/plugin-api/src/index.ts`) has no notion of cost or of where the audio goes. Every provider so far runs on the machine: `whisper-cpp` (`plugins/whisper-cpp`), the shoot stand-in (`standInProvider`, `packages/core/src/shoot/session.ts`) and test fixtures.
- The speech side already has the cost gate: `SpeechProvider.free` / `estimate`, `ConfirmationRequired` with a `confirmation` token and `ConfirmationStale` (ADR 0034 decisions 12 and 20), the key from `OPENAI_API_KEY` only and every network text through `scrub` (decisions 6 and 24, `plugins/openai-speech`).
- The daemon sends audio to a transcription provider from six places: `transcribe` (`transcribeAsset`, which also re-transcribes short windows around over-long words, `recoverLongWords`), `transcribe.verify` (`verifyExport`), the shoot take job (`transcribeTake`), the shoot readiness check, the voiceover misread check and captions (`verifyTakes` through `takeTranscription`), and `analyze` speech.
- Projects pin `@frameshell/whisper-cpp` at the version they installed; pins made before this ADR lack any cost field.
- OpenAI's speech-to-text guide (read 2026-11-18): uploads are at most 25 MB; word timestamps (`response_format=verbose_json`, `timestamp_granularities[]=word`) exist only for `whisper-1`, which the guide marks deprecated with a shutdown on 2027-02-26; the `whisper-1` prompt holds at most 224 tokens. The public pricing page (https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/pricing, read 2026-11-18, no account) lists Whisper at $0.006 per minute.

## Decision

1. **Cloud transcription is an optional plugin.** whisper.cpp stays the default and the only path Frameshell installs by itself. The first cloud provider is `@frameshell/openai-transcription` (`plugins/openai-transcription/`, only `@frameshell/plugin-api`, cloned from the `openai-speech` layout), registering provider id `openai` (`contributes.transcriptionProviders`). A person picks it per call (`transcribe --provider openai`) or per project (`transcription.provider`).
2. **Plugin API: optional cost and place fields** (`remote` proposed by this slice) on `TranscriptionProvider`, the speech shape: `free?: boolean`, `remote?: boolean` (the audio leaves the machine) and `estimate?(audio: { seconds }, options)` → `{ cost, currency, minutes? }`. All optional, so existing providers keep compiling. Unknown cost counts as paid (ADR 0034 decision 12): a provider with neither `estimate` nor `free: true` is paid at an unknown cost. `whisper-cpp` declares `free: true`; so do the stand-ins and fixtures. **Compatibility (proposed, not yet confirmed by the owner):** the provider id `whisper-cpp` counts as local and free whatever its version, because projects pin versions older than this field. A trusted plugin runs with full access to the machine anyway (plugin trust), so this exception weakens nothing: the gate is consent for honest providers, not a sandbox.
3. **`transcribe` refuses until confirmed, every run.** When the provider is paid (an estimate above 0, or unknown cost) or `remote`, `transcribe` extracts the audio as usual, then fails with `ConfirmationRequired` before anything is sent. The message says that **the audio of the asset is uploaded** to the provider (unknown-cost providers: "may be sent off this machine and may cost money"), how long it is, and the estimated cost ("plus short re-checks", decision 6). Data: `{ provider, model, asset, seconds, cost, remote, confirmation }`. The call proceeds only with `yes` (CLI `--yes`); `confirmation` (the token of the refusal, a digest of provider, model, asset content hash and the cost as printed; not the audio source or exact length, which change when an ingest finishes between the ask and the confirmed call) binds the run to what the person saw, and a mismatch fails with `ConfirmationStale`, as in ADR 0034. No consent is stored: the next run asks again. The upload is named in the refusal because the cost is not the only thing the person agrees to; the words of their recording leave the machine.
4. **What leaves the machine:** the 16 kHz mono WAV the host already extracts for transcription of one asset (`.frameshell/cache/audio/`), plus the language code and, for later pieces of a long file, the last words of the piece before as decoding context. No file names, project files, transcripts or people.
5. **Key from the environment only** (ADR 0034 decisions 6 and 24 kept): `OPENAI_API_KEY`, read at each call, used only in the `Authorization` header, never in files, the journal, progress, error data or logs; every text built from the network goes through `scrub`, a hand copy of the `openai-speech` one with a parity test.
6. **Only models with word timestamps qualify.** Slice 1 allows and prices only `whisper-1` ($0.006 per minute, `MODEL_PRICES` with source URL and date). There is no model environment variable; `--model` naming anything else fails before upload. `gpt-4o-transcribe` and its mini sibling give no word timestamps and stay out. The estimate covers the first pass of the whole asset; `recoverLongWords` windows (a few seconds each, at most a handful per file) run inside the confirmed run and are not asked again.
7. **Word timing is the service's.** The plugin returns the words as the service times them and adds a `notes` sentence (shown in `transcribe`'s `warnings`): the times are coarser than whisper.cpp's DTW alignment and cut snapping or `transcribe --verify` should be trusted over them.
8. **Other callers never upload.** `transcribe.verify`, shoot takes and their readiness check, the voiceover misread check and captions, and `analyze` speech take only a local free provider (`free: true`, no `estimate`, not `remote`, or `whisper-cpp`): with any other they refuse (`TranscriptionFailed`, the reason naming `transcribe --yes` and a local provider) or skip as each already does for a missing provider (voiceover verification `skipped`, analyze `speech.reason`, a failed take job). One daemon loader, `localTranscription`, is the only way they load a provider.
9. **Long files (proposed)** are cut by the plugin into pieces under the 25 MB upload limit (10 minutes of 16 kHz mono 16-bit PCM is 19.2 MB), each cut moved to the quietest 100 ms of the 20 s before it, and the word times shifted back to the asset clock.

## Slices

- **CT1 (this one):** decisions 1 to 9: plugin-API fields, the core gate and `transcribe` params `yes`/`confirmation` (`PROTOCOL_VERSION` bump), CLI `transcribe --yes [--confirmation]`, the local-only loader for the other callers, the plugin with stub-server tests, release wiring, skill and docs.
- **Later:** the app's cost and upload dialog for `transcribe` (the app now shows the refusal message); a replacement for `whisper-1` before its 2027-02-26 shutdown (a model with word timestamps, or forced alignment of a timestamp-less transcript); other services as plugins of the same shape.

## Consequences

- A project whose `transcription.provider` is `openai` still transcribes nothing in the background: shoot takes fail, voiceover checks are skipped, `analyze` reports no speech, `transcribe --verify` refuses. The person picks `--provider whisper-cpp` for those.
- A third-party local transcription plugin must declare `free: true`, or its `transcribe` asks for confirmation and the background callers refuse it.
- OpenAI may change the price or retire `whisper-1`; the table states its date and the README says the usage page has the real bill.
