## Sound effects

Frameshell makes short sounds on your computer, offline, with no third-party samples and no licence to read: **click**, **tick**, **pop**, **whoosh**, **riser**, **thump**, **impact**, **shutter**, **glitch**, **braaam**, **swell**, **bell**, **notify** and **sting**.

Ask:

- "A whoosh on each scene change."
- "A click when each check mark appears."
- "Typing sounds while the text types out."
- "One riser before the big reveal."

Each sound lands so its peak hits the picture, a few milliseconds early where the ear expects it, and sits well under the voice. Takes rotate so the same whoosh never plays twice in a row. They go on an audio track called **SFX**.

To hear several sounds before choosing, right-click them in **Files**: **Audition N sounds…** plays them one at a time, at the same loudness if you want.

## Music beds

"Make a minimal music bed with a drop on the logo." Frameshell can compose **synthwave**, **lo-fi** or **minimal** music on your computer: an intro, builds, a drop on a marker, and a final hit that rings. It comes with an exact beat grid, so cuts and sounds snap to it. See [Music](../music/).

## A cleaner voice

A recording from a phone or laptop gets better with one setting: ask "clean up my voice", or turn it on in the **Voice** section of the inspector. It removes rumble, lowers the background noise and evens the level. Do it before music goes under.

## Loudness

For shorts your AI sets the export to -14 LUFS, the level Instagram, TikTok and YouTube Shorts play at. The export keeps peaks under -1 dB.
