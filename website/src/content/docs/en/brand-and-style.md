## Capture your brand

Ask: "Capture our brand from example.com." Frameshell opens the page in its own browser (public pages only, no sign-in) and saves the logo candidates, icons, the share image, a picture of the page, its colours and open-licensed fonts under `assets/brand/`. It can also crop sections of the page ("the pricing table") as sharp pictures for push-ins on your real product.

Your AI lists what it found and asks which logo and colours are yours before using anything. It never redraws a logo or invents product screens.

Product behind a login, or designed in Figma? Export the frames as SVG into a folder and ask your AI to import it.

## Your style

The project's style is a file, `style.json`, that every video reads: colours, fonts, type sizes, how things move, sound levels, platforms and their safe zones, rules, and a list of things never to do.

- "Save my colours and fonts in the style."
- "Rule: never bounce the text."
- "Save this style for my next projects."

Notes you give once become rules your AI follows every time. A style saved by name can start your next project.

## Fonts and credits

Only fonts whose licence allows it are copied into the project. Everything from outside (fonts, music, textures, logos) carries its source and licence, and your AI lists the credits for your video description before export.
