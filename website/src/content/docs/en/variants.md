## Formats

One edit can go out as horizontal 16:9, vertical 9:16, square 1:1 and portrait 4:5, all from the same cuts. The preview has a format switcher; a caption or card can sit differently in one format without touching the others.

Ask: "Add a vertical format", "In the square version, move the title to the center", "Export every format".

## Batch variants

When the edit is finished and only data changes (a title or a price per product, the voiceover and captions per language, a different clip in the same slot), your AI can make a batch: a table of rows, one video each.

- "Make this ad in English, Spanish and Portuguese."
- "One version per product, with its name and price."
- "Show me what each row changes before rendering."

Rows that fail can be rendered again on their own; a changed row renders again by itself.

## When not to batch

When versions need different timing, cuts or structure, they are separate edits. And batch is never a way to mass-produce episodes: platforms such as YouTube demonetise repetitive content. A series needs its own story each time.
