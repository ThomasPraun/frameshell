For podcasts, interviews, panels and recorded Zoom, Meet or Teams calls: one file per person, cameras with separate microphones, or one gallery-view recording with everyone in tiles.

## What to ask

- "Treat it like a multicam shoot and show whoever is talking."
- "Interview style: long shots on the answers."
- "Remove the silences first."
- "Split the call recording into its tiles."
- "Make a vertical version that follows each face."

## How it works

1. **Sync.** The files are lined up by their sound. Your AI checks the sync before anything else.
2. **Cut the silences**, so the cutting sees the final length.
3. **Pick a style.** *Interview*: long solos on the answers, a cut to the listener now and then. *Podcast calm*: long holds, two-shots on banter. *Panel dynamic*: short shots for people trading quickly.
4. **Direct.** A director cuts between solos, two-shots and wides by who speaks. The same rules always give the same cut.
5. **Review the doubtful shots.** The director marks shots it was not sure of; your AI reads what is said there and fixes them.

## Correct it yourself

- Open **Angles** in the sidebar: every camera plays side by side. Click one to cut to it at the playhead.
- Press <kbd>1</kbd> to <kbd>9</kbd> while it plays to cut to that angle; <kbd>Shift</kbd> adds an angle to the shot.
- Your shots are kept: when the AI directs again, it keeps what you chose and redoes the rest.

## Who is who

The **People** view links each name to its voice, camera and face, so subtitles show the speaker and "show me where Ana speaks" works. See [People](../people/).

## Vertical

A 9:16 version of 16:9 cameras keeps a narrow strip of each picture. Ask your AI to reframe: it finds the faces and follows them. The first time it downloads a face model.

## Screen shares

A recording of a shared screen is an angle too. Mark it as a screen and the director shows it while a slide changes or a demo moves, and nobody is speaking.
