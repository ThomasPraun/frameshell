For launch films, product promos, animated explainers and story-led shorts.

## Start

On the welcome screen choose **New video from an idea…**. Give it a name, its formats (horizontal, vertical, square, 4:5: all from the same edit), a rough length, and optionally a web page it is about. Frameshell makes the project and gives you a first prompt for your AI, with a **Copy** button for an AI app outside Frameshell. The brief is kept in `brief.md`.

## How it goes

Your AI works like a director with checkpoints. It stops and waits for you at each one:

1. **Three storylines**, each different, with one recommended. You pick.
2. **Assumptions and costs**, with a free alternative next to every paid item: tools, stock, music, voices, fonts. Nothing paid without your yes.
3. **A style guide**: your colours, fonts, how things move, platforms. See [Brand and style](../brand-and-style/).
4. **A storyboard** in bars of music, adding up to the length. You give the OK.
5. **Shots, board and animatic**: one still per shot, then a rough draft for the pacing.
6. **Cuts, sound and music** on the beat, with the music's last hit on the end card.
7. **Critique and finals**: the film is checked against the brief before you see it, then exported in every format.

## What to ask

- "A 30-second launch film for our app, vertical and horizontal."
- "A 60-second animated story told with my voice."
- "Make it like this reference video": see [Make it like this](../reference-video/).

## Tips

- Give your site's address: your AI can capture your real logo, colours and fonts instead of guessing.
- Generated scenes need the HyperFrames, Remotion or Blender plugin. Your AI offers them; you choose.
