## Transitions

A transition belongs to a clip's edge; clips stay where they are and the length of the video does not change. Sound crossfades with it.

| Kind | Use it when |
|---|---|
| **Dissolve** | One moment blends into the next |
| **Fade** | Into or out of black: start, end, a pause in time |
| **Push** | A new topic at the same level |
| **Wipe** | Something already sweeps across the screen |
| **Iris** | Closing on a detail or a reveal |
| **Zoom, slats, pixel grid** | A strong change of scene, sparingly |

Ask: "dissolve between the two interviews", "fade out at the end", "a push to the next section". Never one on every cut: a hard cut is right most of the time.

In the app, select a clip and set its transitions in the **inspector**: kind, direction, length. The timeline marks every transition.

## Zooms and moves

Any clip value can change over time with keyframes: scale, position, opacity, volume. Ask "slowly push in while I say the key line" or "zoom in 10 % on the reveal". Your AI keeps zooms gentle and gives each one a reason.

In the inspector, keyframes have an ease (how the value moves to the next one). In the preview, drag the selected clip's handles to move or scale it.

## Reframing for vertical

A 16:9 shot in a 9:16 video shows a narrow strip. Ask "reframe it for vertical around my face": Frameshell finds the face and follows it. When there is no face to find, tell your AI where the subject stands.

## Music ducking

Music can dip by itself while someone speaks: "duck the music under the voice". See [Music](../music/).
