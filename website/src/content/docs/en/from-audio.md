## Start

On the welcome screen choose **New from video, audio or image…** and pick the audio file. The form asks whether the video is **horizontal (16:9)** or **vertical (9:16)**. Leave **Transcribe what is said** on.

## Three levels

| Level | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Simple** | The audio, a still background and subtitles that follow it |
| **Medium** | Plus section titles, highlighted key words and pictures |
| **Explainer** | Plus an animation, diagram or b-roll per scene |

## What to ask

- "Make a simple video of this episode with subtitles."
- "Make an explainer of this talk."
- "Cut the long pauses first."

## How an explainer is made

1. **The audio is edited first**: pauses and retakes out. Pictures do not move with their words, so they come after.
2. **A scene plan** is written to `scripts/` as plain text: one scene per section of the audio, with the words it covers and what is on screen. Open it in the editor, change anything, or tell your AI what to change.
3. **Nothing is built until you say go.** Generated scenes are slow to redo; a plan is cheap.
4. **Scenes are built** one by one and linked to their part of the plan. The scene band over the timeline shows which part of the plan each stretch plays.

Animated scenes need the HyperFrames or Remotion plugin; see [Titles and animations](../titles-and-animations/).
