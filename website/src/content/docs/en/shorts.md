## What to ask

- "Make a vertical short of the best minute for Reels and TikTok."
- "Turn this take into a reel: tight cuts, captions with the key word, two or three graphics."
- "Make three shorts from this episode."
- "Also export it as 4:5 and 1:1."

Your AI asks a few questions first, each with a recommendation: which take, which platform, how many graphics, whether you want music, and whether you want to watch after every pass or only approve the key points.

## How it is built

A good reel is built in passes, and each pass is a step in History you can watch and revert:

1. **Words**: transcribe, and fix names and numbers.
2. **Cut**: pauses and false starts out, reel-tight.
3. **Captions**: big, at chest height, with a key word.
4. **Graphics plan**: a table of what is said, when, and what appears. Markers on the timeline show the plan before any graphic exists. You approve it first.
5. **Zooms**: gentle push-ins on the key phrases.
6. **Sound**: voice clean-up, effects on the hits, music under the voice that ducks while you speak.
7. **Export**: a quick draft for review, then the final.

## A short from a long video

A short is a second timeline in the same project, made from the same footage with no copy. Make as many as you want. Horizontal footage is reframed to follow the face in the vertical version.

## A word behind your head

A big word between you and the background, so you stand in front of it. Ask "put 'NEVER' behind me at the start". Frameshell cuts you out of the background (it takes about 2 minutes per minute of video, once), and the preview shows the word in front until that is ready.

## The cover

Platforms use the first frame as the thumbnail. Ask for "a cover with the word 'free'": Frameshell picks the frame where you say it, puts the word over it big, and checks it is readable at thumbnail size.

## Tips

- Keep text out of the top, bottom and right edges, where the platform puts its buttons. "Check the safe zones" shows them.
- A note like "it covers my chin" is a rule for the whole video, and your AI applies it everywhere.
- Rules you want for every reel ("never bounce the text", your colours) can be saved in your [style](../brand-and-style/).
