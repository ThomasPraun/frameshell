## Start

- **From pictures**: on the welcome screen choose **New from video, audio or image…** and pick one or more images. The picture takes the size of the first one, and each image is a layer, the first at the bottom.
- **From a frame of your video**: in a video project, **File > New Image from Frame…** (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>/<kbd>Cmd</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>S</kbd>, or right-click the preview). Choose a size, for example a YouTube thumbnail (1280x720), square or vertical. It opens a new image project beside the video's, with that frame as the background. The video is not changed.

## What to ask

- "Make a YouTube thumbnail: my face on the right, 'NEVER AGAIN' big on the left."
- "Put our logo in the top corner."
- "Make the background darker so the text reads."
- "Export it as JPEG."

Text and shapes are made as small compositions, so each stays editable: point at it with the **Pick** tool and ask for a change.

## The Layers panel

An image project has no timeline. The **Layers** panel lists one row per layer, top first: select, rename, opacity, move up or down, delete. In the preview, drag a selected layer to move it and its handles to scale it. <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>/<kbd>Cmd</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd> undoes.

## Export

**Export image** in the preview header saves PNG, JPEG or WebP.
