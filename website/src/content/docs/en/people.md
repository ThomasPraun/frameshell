The **People** view in the sidebar links each person to their voice in the transcripts, their camera angle and their face.

## What to ask

- "The first voice is Ana, the second is Ben."
- "Find Ana in the other recordings too."
- "Scan the faces in these videos."
- "When does Ben speak?"
- "Show me every moment Ana is on screen."

## Suggestions

Once one voice is named, Frameshell suggests the same voice in your other files. After a face scan it groups the same face across videos, and suggests who is on screen while someone speaks. Suggestions show at the top of the People view with **Confirm** and **Dismiss**; nothing is linked without a confirmation.

## In the app

- Click a person: their words are selected in the transcript and their stretches on the timeline (**Speaks** or **On screen**).
- Rename a speaker in the transcript view and the person is renamed too; subtitles show the new name.
- <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>/<kbd>Cmd</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd> in the People view undoes the last change, the AI's included.

## Privacy

`people.json` keeps names and links only. Voice and face prints are cached in the project's hidden `.frameshell/` folder, can be made again, and never leave your computer.
