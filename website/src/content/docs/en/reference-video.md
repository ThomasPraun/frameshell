"Make it like this" works when the reference is measured instead of guessed.

## What to ask

- "Here is a reel I like: make mine with the same pace."
- "Analyze this ad and tell me what makes it work."
- "Compare my export with the reference."

## What is measured

A one-minute video takes about half a minute. Frameshell writes down:

- **Cutting**: number of shots, cuts per minute, the shortest and longest, jump cuts and punch-ins told apart from hard cuts.
- **Look**: palette, brightness, contrast, saturation, warmth.
- **Movement**: how busy the picture is over time, and the eases.
- **Graphics**: how much of the frame text and graphics cover.
- **Sound**: loudness, tempo, voice against music, effects, sections.

Your AI labels each point it uses as **measured**, **estimated** or **a decision**, so you know what is a fact and what is taste.

## Compare

After you export, ask to compare: a side-by-side sheet and how close the rhythm and energy are.

## What it never does

It takes the grammar (pace, rhythm, framing, colour) and never the content: no characters, designs, faces, copy or script from the reference.
