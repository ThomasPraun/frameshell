This is the whole path, start to finish. The welcome screen shows the same four steps under **How it goes**.

## 1. Connect your AI

If you have not yet, [connect your AI](../connect-your-ai/). You do it once per computer.

## 2. Bring your footage

On the welcome screen, choose **New from video, audio or image…** or drop your files on the window. A short form asks:

- **Project name** and **Folder**: where the project goes.
- **Keep a copy or use the files where they are.** A copy is safer; using them in place saves disk space, but then do not move or delete them.
- **Transcribe what is said.** Leave it on for anything with speech: subtitles, cutting pauses and editing by words all start from the transcript. The first time it downloads the transcription engine and model (about 600 MB) and asks first.
- For an audio file with no picture, whether the video will be **horizontal (16:9)** or **vertical (9:16)**.

No footage yet? **New video from an idea…** starts from a description, and the [Shoot view](../shoot/) records you with a teleprompter.

## 3. Ask for a cut, then correct it

In your AI app, describe what you want, like you would to an editor:

- "Cut the pauses and the false starts."
- "Add subtitles, two words at a time."
- "Make a vertical short of the best minute for Reels."

Each change shows in the preview and on the timeline as it happens. Fix anything by hand: drag a clip, trim an edge, click a struck word in the transcript to bring it back. The AI reads what you changed and carries on from there. [Editing by hand](../editing-by-hand/) has every gesture.

## 4. Export

Ask: "Export it for YouTube" or "Export the short". The video lands in the project's `exports/` folder. Want to finish in another editor? **File > Export Premiere XML…** or **File > Export OpenTimelineIO…** hands the timeline over; see [Export](../export/).
